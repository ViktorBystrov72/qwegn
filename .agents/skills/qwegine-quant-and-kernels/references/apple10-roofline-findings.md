# Apple10 (M5 Max, 40-Core GPU) Measured Kernel Findings & Prerequisites

Consult before proposing or re-implementing GEMM, GEMV, MoE, NAX, or speculative-verify optimizations on Apple10 GPUs.

## 2. Proven Positive Tile & Fusion Patterns

* **Apple10 `_bm32` (`32`-token, `128`-thread) `f32` GEMM tiles (`HQ4_G32`, `AQ4_G{32,64}`, `AQ8_G32`, `SHVQ4_G{32,64}`):** Dispatch `_bm32` when `rows == 32 || (rows > 32 && rows < 64 && K / 2 >= N)` and `epi <= 3`. Eliminates `50%` idle row lanes vs `64`-row `_bm` (`256` threads), yielding **`1.22x–1.52x`** isolated operator speedups (`N=2048..2560, K=512..6144`) and **`1.0112x`** `32`-token CLI prefill speedup on `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B-hq4g32` with `0`-ULP bit-exactness.
* **FlashNext / Edge0-145B Prefill Wave 1+2 (`v0.20.0`):**
  * **MoE tail tile compaction (`qw_moe_tile.metalh`, `qwg_moe_group_pairs_f32`, `HQ4`/`AQ`/`SHVQ` batched NAX):** Packs full `64`-row blocks (`code=3`) + compact `16/32`-row tail block (`code=1/2`) in `tileR[t]` across all 512 experts, eliminating wasted `bm=64` tiles on tail tokens.
  * **0-ULP PLE & HC fusion (`qwple.metal`, `qwhc.metal`, `FNGPULayer.swift`):** Fuses `keyValueBank` projection (`W+H`), `qwg_ple_fused_gate_norm` (bit-exact `UInt32.bitPattern` match to unfused sequence), `qwg_hc_inject_norm` (`qwg_hc_inject_norm_core<false>` without unneeded `WHT32`), and `qwg_ple_conv` in-place residual add (`stream`).
  * **Fused `inProjBA32` + strided conv prep (`FNGPULayer.swift`, `qwgdn_f32.metal`):** Concatenates `in_proj_b` + `in_proj_a` into `ba32` for one `[T, 2*VH, H]` GEMV and fast `h / 3u` head mapping when `VHEADS == kh * 3u`.
  * **QSA `simdgroup_half8x8` scorers (`qwqsa.metal`, `qwemeans_coarse_scorers.metal`) + full-`T` `chunkMax[516]` radix top-k + `gqa8` Sparse NAX (`qwattn_sparse_nax.metal`):** Eliminates half the `xchg` barriers and exponent passes when `gqa <= 8` (`24/2` on `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` / `Edge0-145B`) and accelerates `32K–256K` QSA selection.
  * **Sliver-free outer prefill chunking (`FNGPUPrefillStack.deferredSubchunkRows`, `FNGPUSession.prefillStepChunk`):** Locks `prefillStepChunk` once per prompt and defers partial outer-slice remainders (`4032 x N`) instead of shrinking chunk sizes or emitting small tail slivers (`0` steady-state allocations).
  * **64-bit `>16 GiB` PLE gather offsets (`qwple.metal`, `FNGPUDecodeStack.pleTargetWordOffset`, `93893f4a`):** Widens `targetFloatOffsets` in `qwg_ple_vq2_gather` / `qwg_ple_vq4_gather` to `device const ulong*` (`UInt64`), fixing `UInt32` float-index `SIGTRAP` overflows when activation + KV arenas exceed `16 GiB` (`Edge0-145B` `1M q8 --no-stream-ngrams` verified at `102.99 GiB` planned, `0` allocs). Full `v0.20.0` peaks: `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` **`3,034.8 tok/s`** `@8K` (`+29.17%` `@65K`), `Edge0-145B` **`2,572.0 tok/s`** `@4K` / **`2,568.4`** `@8K` (`+10.24%` MTP-3 **`67.59 tok/s`**, `300K` YaRN **`1,885.5 tok/s`** `3/3` needles).
* **HCA, NoPE MLA, Multi-Row GEMV (`M=2..8`) & Sparse-MoE `.hybrid` Wave (`ec3359d3..2ac33efd`):**
  * **HCA NAX prefill + `24 MiB` SLC-chunked grouped `wo_a` (`qwattn_hca_out.metal`, `qwattn_hca_nax.metal`, `QWHCAAttnOperator+Snapshot.swift`) & NoPE MLA `h32` (`qwattn_mla_nax_h32.metal`, `qw_mla_nax_common.metalh`):** Cuts `hca.grouped_wo_a` **`-36.8%`** (`333.1 -> 210.4 ms`, `45.2 TFLOPS`) at `0`-ULP parity, `hca.attn` `117.4 -> 110.3 ms`, and NoPE `mla.sparse_attn` `460.2 -> 447.7 ms`.
  * **Disjoint prefill scratch aliasing + dead `WY`/`skipOut32` pruning (`DriverConfig.swift`, `DriverInitPhases.swift`, `qwgemm_moe_nax_f16.metal`):** Cuts `fixed_scratch` by `410 MiB` (`ERNIE-5.5`) and `1.63 GiB` (`GLM-5.3` `c=4096`).
  * **Multi-row verify GEMV (`M=2..8`, splitting `rem == 9 -> 5 + 4`) & `256`-thread latent/`silu` TGs (`qw_dequant_multi_b.metalh`, `qw_dequant_shvq.metalh`, `qwrouter.metal`, `QWMTPBatchedMLP.swift`, `QWHead.swift`, `QWGemmF32.swift`):** Halves verify passes across `M=5..8`, cutting `rows=6` `ERNIE-5.5` verify `gpu_busy` by `~4.4 ms` (`-7.2%`/round).
  * **Chunk-first UMA admission + `1 GiB` `MAP_SHARED` embed reserve + KDA conditioning (`>= 1e-3`) + sparse-MoE `.hybrid` `neuralCap=1` (`DriverResidency.swift`, `QWGDNReplayCheckpoint.swift`, `QWMTPAdaptiveDepth.swift`, `QWTokenRecycler.swift`):** End-to-end vs `c1be56c1`: `ERNIE-5.5` `d=0 4K` **`+15.6%`** prefill (`1136.5 tok/s`) / **`+6.3%`** decode (`32.96 tok/s`), `d=5 code` **`+25.6%`** decode (`64.19 tok/s`, needle `76.38 tok/s`); `GLM-5.3` `c=4096` `929.7` prefill / `43.63` decode, `d=5 code c=1024` **`+24.4%`** (`47.89 tok/s`), `d=5 short c=1024` **`+57.3%`** (`44.42 tok/s`).
* **FA4-Inspired Attention Wave (`v0.31.0`, `perf/attn-fa4-w2`):**
  * **Split-K NAX QSA decode & verify (`qwattn_sparse_nax.metal` `qwg_attn_sparse_gated_nax_split_{f32,f16,q8,q4}` + `qwg_attn_sparse_gated_nax_combine`, `QWQSACacheState`, `QWQSAEncoders`, `FNGPULayer`):** Replaces low-occupancy `8`-TG (`20%` core) scalar `gqa3` for `T <= 8` with wave-filling Split-K NAX FP16 `matmul2d` while keeping prefill on exact `xchg[2][256]`, lifting `Qwen3.8-Flash-Next` decode **`+10.70%`** (`49.91 -> 55.25 tok/s`).
  * **MBSA NAX `tiled32` selector, compact 32-lane decode & LPT prefill (`qwattn_mbsa.metal`, `qwattn_mbsa_nax.metal`, `QWMBSAAttnOperator`):** Upgrades `qwg_mbsa_select_blocks_tiled32` to 128-thread `BaseNAXFrag::mma_k32` + in-register row max, shrinks decode to 32-lane `ELEMS=4` `8 KiB` TG SRAM with hoisted `sAbsKey`/`sPk`/`sPv` + 8-lane `mbsa_fwht32_h4`, and adds LPT prefill + wave-aligned `targetSplits`, lifting `Maxwell-V1-Flash` decode **`+9.09%`** (`47.64 -> 51.85 tok/s`) and prefill **`+2.67%`** (`4,515.2 -> 4,635.9 tok/s`).
  * **Wave-aligned `QWAttnDecode.splits(forKeys:kvHeads:gpuCores:tokensPerSplit:)` + `qwattn_decode_general.metal` hoisting:** Keeps `>= 8` short-context floor (`keys <= 512`), hoists TG `sPk`/`sPv`, guards online softmax (`Mb > m && m > -1e29f`), and fuses single-exp combine scaling, lifting `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` decode **`+2.23%`** (`123.08 -> 125.53 tok/s`) and `Apex-35B-V1.0` decode **`+2.01%`** (`122.63 -> 127.18 tok/s`, `0`-ULP).
* **Full-Slice Q4 Multi-Head LSA (`v0.35.0`, `qwlsa_mh.metal`, `qwlsa_mh_select.metal`, `qwlsa_mh_common.metalh`, `QWLSAConfig.adaptiveBudget`):** Per-head Q4 full-`headDim` (`128`) signatures with 2-stage coarse->fine `bqTile=4` register-LSE scoring and context-scaled budgets (`4,096` at `>= 98,304`, `8,192` at `>= 196,608`, `16,384` at `>= 786,432`; maintaining `>= 3.1%` floor up to `256K` and `>= 1.56%` at `512K–1M`) achieve **`12/12` (`100%`)** bit-exact needle recall across `8K–1M` on `Inkling-2-Mini` (`2,017 / 33.9 tok/s` at `1M`, `3.74× / 4.51×` vs dense). Declare Metal `threadgroup` buffers in `kernel void` entrypoints and pass `threadgroup T*` pointers into helper functions (Metal rejects `threadgroup` declarations inside non-`kernel` helpers).

## 3. Structural Prerequisites for Deferred Ideas

* **Tree-Structured Speculative Verification:** Requires (1) parent-bitmask causal attention in paged verify (`verifyTokensLocked` currently assigns linear `pos + qi + 1` causal prefixes where sibling branches attend to one another) and (2) parent-slot-indexed GDN recurrent state and PLE conv history rings (currently row-ordered linear rollback tapes).
* **Adaptive Probability-Mass (`tau`) Top-$k$ Expert Pruning:** Do not substitute cross-row `--moe-spec-budget` (`budget=8` drops `Qwen3.6-35B-A3B` `llmprobe` `code-10` from `9/10` to `1/10`). Any per-token `tau` cutoff requires router probability-mass trace analysis, an offline logit/KL oracle, full `llmprobe`, and `3`-needle passkey gates before merge.
